Key takeaways from the updated Berlin Rent Index 2026

The Berlin Mietspiegel 2026 replaces the 2024 edition. It is an official reference for comparable net cold rent, not a measure of asking rents or GermanyRent’s reported warm rents.

The headline figure rose to €7.71 per m²

The official 2026 overall median net cold rent is €7.71 per m². The 2024 overall figure was €7.21 per m², a €0.50 per m² increase, or roughly 6.9% at the citywide headline level. This is not a flat 6.9% change for every home. The table value for a specific home still depends on construction period, size, residential location, and other characteristics.

What the 2026 update is based on

The new qualified Mietspiegel covers about 1.6 million relevant homes and is based on approximately 17,000 rent and equipment records. It reflects rents that were newly agreed or changed within the last six years, with a reference date of 1 September 2025. The official online service helps find the relevant table context for a particular address.

Location classifications were updated

The share of addresses classified as simple residential location is 29.4%, up from 28.5% in 2024. Medium location is 49.9%, down from 52.1%. Good location is 20.7%, up from 19.4%. These changes reflect an updated classification process, so they should not be read as a simple statement that individual neighbourhoods improved or declined.

Energy has a clearer role in the orientation guidance

The 2026 orientation guidance includes a tiered energy indicator. Lower energy use can count positively and higher energy use negatively when placing a home within its table range. It is part of the wider assessment, not a shortcut to a single correct rent.

What the Mietspiegel does not cover

Its direct scope is non-price-regulated flats in Berlin multi-family buildings completed by 31 December 2024. It excludes, among other things, owner-occupied homes, one and two-family homes, newer homes completed from 2025, and price-regulated or publicly subsidised homes. Check the official scope before relying on it for a particular tenancy.
