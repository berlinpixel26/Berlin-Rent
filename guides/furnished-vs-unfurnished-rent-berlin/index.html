Furnished vs unfurnished rent in Berlin

Furnishing can make a home easier to move into, but it can also make price comparisons less clear.

Do not compare the headline price alone

A furnished home may include furniture, kitchen equipment, appliances, or a shorter-term arrangement. An unfurnished home can require more upfront spending, but the monthly cost may not include the same conveniences. Compare the total offer: warm rent, deposit, what is supplied, and which costs still sit outside the rent.

Use like-for-like filters

When using GermanyRent, apply the furnished filter before comparing nearby pins. Then match the room count and whether the report refers to a whole flat or a shared room. This will not make every home identical, but it removes the largest sources of false comparison.

Check the contract details

Ask for an inventory if furniture is included. Clarify which operating costs are part of the warm rent and whether there are separate payments for electricity, internet, or other services. If you need legal guidance on a furnished rental, use an official source or tenant organisation rather than relying on a general rent comparison.
