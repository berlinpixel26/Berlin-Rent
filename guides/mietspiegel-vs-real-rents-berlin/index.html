What the Mietspiegel is for

The official Berlin Mietspiegel is a qualified reference for local comparable rent. It uses defined characteristics including home size, equipment, location, and building age. Open the official Berlin Mietspiegel 2026 when you need formal context for a specific home.

What GermanyRent adds

GermanyRent shows voluntary, community-reported warm rents linked to map points. These reports are not a representative sample or proof that an amount is legally permitted. They can help you explore the range renters report nearby and compare factors such as furnishing and when the current rent began.

Use both without mixing them up

  • Use the Mietspiegel for official methodology and formal context.
  • Use GermanyRent to explore community-reported monthly costs and local variation.
  • Compare only reports that are genuinely similar to the home you are considering.
  • Do not use one pin or one average as a legal conclusion.
