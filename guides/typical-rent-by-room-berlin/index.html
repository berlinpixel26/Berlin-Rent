Typical Berlin rent by room

Room count is a useful first filter, but it is never the whole story.

Start with similar homes

Compare one-room homes with one-room homes, two-room homes with two-room homes, and so on. This removes the most obvious mismatch when looking at reported rent figures.

Then add the context

Even homes with the same number of rooms can differ substantially. The most useful comparisons also consider district, exact location, furnished or unfurnished status, shared or whole-flat arrangement, size, and when the current rent began.

Why Rent since matters

A tenant who began paying their current rent years ago can have a very different figure from a recent tenant in a similar home. The date helps you understand how current the reported price is.

Use ranges and sample sizes

A typical or median reported rent is a reference, not a guarantee. Check how many pins contribute to the result and whether the homes resemble the one you need. Community data is most useful when it is read as reported experience, not certainty.
