How to compare a Berlin rent offer

A rent is only comparable when the homes behind the numbers are comparable too.

Start with the full monthly cost

Use the warm rent to decide whether a home fits your monthly budget. Then ask which costs are included and which remain separate, such as electricity, internet, parking, or a broadcasting contribution. A lower cold rent does not automatically mean a lower monthly cost.

Compare the right peer group

First match room count, then furnishing and housing type. A furnished room in a shared flat should not be measured against an unfurnished whole flat. Location matters, but district alone is broad. Use nearby pins as context, then check the home itself: size, condition, floor, building type, and what is included.

Rent since changes the story

A current tenant may have started paying their rent years ago, while a similar home was newly rented this year. GermanyRent collects Rent since so you can see how recently the reported amount began. It is useful context, not a verdict on whether an offer is fair or legally permitted.

Use the map as a shortlist

  1. Filter for a similar room count and housing type.
  2. Look at several pins, not one outlier.
  3. Check the Rent since date on each comparable pin.
  4. Keep deposit and move-in costs separate from monthly rent.
  5. Use the official Mietspiegel or tenant advice when you need a legal assessment.
