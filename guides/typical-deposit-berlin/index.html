Move-in costs

How much deposit is typical in Berlin?

A home can fit your monthly budget but still be hard to move into because of upfront costs.

Think beyond the monthly rent

The deposit, or Kaution, is often one of the largest payments you make when moving. A realistic budget may also include the first rent payment, moving, furniture, and connection or setup costs.

Compare similar homes

One city-wide deposit figure is rarely enough. Compare reported deposits between homes with a similar room count, furnishing status, type of arrangement, district, and rent level. This gives you a more practical picture of what you may need to save.

Questions to clarify before signing

  • What is the exact deposit amount?
  • When is it due and how will it be documented?
  • Which costs are monthly and which are one-off?
  • What does the agreement say about return of the deposit?

Keep a written record of the home's condition at move-in. It provides a clearer reference at the end of the tenancy.

Use reported deposits responsibly

GermanyRent shows what renters have reported, not a legal assessment of what a landlord may charge. Read the contract carefully and seek qualified tenant advice if a term is unclear.

Reported deposit information helps renters plan the full cost of moving, not only the monthly price.
