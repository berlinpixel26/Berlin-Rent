Warm rent vs cold rent in Berlin

The monthly number in a listing is only useful when you know what it includes.

What is cold rent?

Cold rent, or Kaltmiete, is the base rent for the home before most building operating costs are added. It helps compare the price of similar homes, but it is not usually the full monthly payment.

What is warm rent?

Warm rent, or Warmmiete, combines cold rent with advance payments for certain operating costs, often called Nebenkosten. Depending on the building and agreement, these may include heating, water, waste collection, cleaning, or shared building costs. Electricity and internet may still be separate.

How to compare a listing

  • Start with warm rent when checking your monthly budget.
  • Ask what the operating-cost advance includes.
  • List separately billed costs, especially electricity and internet.
  • Separate monthly costs from one-off move-in costs such as deposit.
  • Compare homes with a similar room count, furnishing type, and rent-since date.

Why GermanyRent uses warm rent

Two homes with similar cold rent can have different operating costs. Reported warm rent gives renters a more practical starting point when comparing what they may pay each month.
