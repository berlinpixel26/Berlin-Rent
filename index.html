📘 How GermanyRent works

Explore real rents, then make the map more useful with yours.

1. Explore rents around Berlin

  • Search for a street, building, or postcode to move around the map.
  • Tap a blue rent pill to see the reported rent, home details, and renter ratings.
  • Use the filter icon to narrow results by room count, warm rent, district, furnishing, and type.

2. Share your rent

  • Choose 📍 Add my Rent, search for your address or place the red pin directly on the map.
  • Drag the red pin to fine-tune the public location, then enter the essential rent details.
  • Your pin is published immediately. You can add further optional details afterwards.

3. Stay in control

  • No name, account, or email is needed. Email is optional and only used if you ask for a notification.
  • From the same browser, you can edit or delete the pin you submitted.
  • Use 📊 Stats for the map’s live aggregate picture and 🚇 to show Berlin rail transit.

GermanyRent is community data, not an official rent index or legal advice. Compare homes with similar size, type, and rent-effective date. See how GermanyRent data works.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is this really anonymous?

Yes. A name, account, and email are not required to submit a pin. If you choose to provide an email for your own notification, it is never displayed publicly or shared.

📍 Is my exact address public?

The location you confirm with the red pin is shown on the public map. Use the map to choose the level of precision you are comfortable sharing, and avoid including identifying details in the optional note.

🧾 Where does the rent data come from?

From renters sharing what they pay. Pins go live immediately, so the map reflects community-reported data rather than listings or asking prices.

🚩 Are submissions checked or moderated?

They are not pre-moderated. Anyone can flag a suspicious pin using 🚩 Report in the rent detail card. A pin is removed after five reports.

✏️ Can I edit or delete a rent I submitted?

Yes, from the same browser and device used to add it. GermanyRent keeps a private edit key in that browser; there is no account or login to recover it elsewhere.

💶 What do “current warm rent” and “rent applicable since” mean?

Current warm rent is the monthly amount you pay now, including applicable service charges. Choose when this amount began. If your rent changed after moving in, use the month of the most recent change.

🤝 Is GermanyRent connected to landlords, brokers, or listing platforms?

No. GermanyRent is an independent community project. It does not sell listings, charge brokerage fees, or give paid placement to anyone.

🚇 Why do transit lines only appear when I zoom in?

Berlin’s rail map is easiest to read at street level. Zoom closer after enabling the 🚇 layer.

👋 About GermanyRent

A renter-built map for more transparent housing decisions.

💡 Why it exists

Rental prices are difficult to understand when the only visible number is an asking price. GermanyRent helps tenants see what people report paying in real homes and neighbourhoods, so they can choose where to live based on their needs and preferences.

👨‍💻 Built by Nikhil Singh

I'm building GermanyRent.de to turn opaque rental prices into useful, community-powered information. The goal is to give tenants a clearer starting point before they move, negotiate, or sign.

🔗 Stay in touch on LinkedIn

🗺️ How the map stays useful

Every rent pin is contributed by a renter. The more people share, the more helpful the map becomes for comparing a move, understanding a neighbourhood, or preparing for a rent conversation.

🧭 Our principles

  • Renter-first: free to use, with no account required.
  • Independent: no brokers, landlords, or paid map placement.
  • Community-led: pins are reported by renters and suspicious entries can be flagged.
  • Useful context: rent amount, room count, furnishing, district, and the date that rent began all matter.

🌱 Where we are going

GermanyRent starts with Berlin. The ambition is a clearer, community-built view of rents in cities across Germany—one useful contribution at a time.

GermanyRent is not a property portal, rent index, or legal service. It is a practical community reference to use alongside your own research.
