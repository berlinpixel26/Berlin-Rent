📘 How to Use berlin.rent

A quick guide to the map.
  • Click anywhere on the map to add a rent pin at that exact spot, or use the 📍 Add a Rent button for an approximate location.
  • Click any pill marker (e.g. "2BHK · €850") to see the full rent details, ratings, and notes.
  • Use the 🔍 search bar to jump to a district, and the filter icon next to it to narrow results by bedrooms, price, district, or type.
  • The 📊 Stats button shows live aggregate numbers across all listed rents.
  • The 🚇 button toggles a dedicated transit (rail/S-Bahn/U-Bahn) map style.
  • Everything you submit is 100% anonymous — no login, no email required.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really anonymous?
Yes — we don't ask for your name, email, or login to submit a rent pin.

Where does the rent data come from?
Directly from renters in Berlin, submitted through this map. This prototype currently shows sample/demo data.

Can I edit or remove a pin I submitted?
Not yet — this is planned for a future version once accounts (optional) are introduced.

Is this affiliated with any broker or landlord?
No. berlin.rent is independent and never charges brokerage fees.

Why can't I see transit lines at low zoom?
Zoom in closer — the transit map style is easiest to read at street level.
